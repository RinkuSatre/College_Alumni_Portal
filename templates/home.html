About College Alumni Portal

The College Alumni Portal is a platform that connects current students with alumni. It helps alumni stay connected with the college and provides students with career guidance, job opportunities, networking, and event participation.

Through this portal, students can communicate with alumni, apply for jobs, participate in events, and grow their professional network.
